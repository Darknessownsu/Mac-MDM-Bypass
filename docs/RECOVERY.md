# Supported recovery guidance

This toolkit does not change system state.

- For unexpected enrollment, verify purchase records and contact the seller or managing organization. Ask the organization to release the device through its authorized management workflow.
- For Activation Lock, use Apple-supported ownership verification or account recovery.
- For unexpected security posture, consult Apple Support or the organization's administrator. Do not disable protections to resolve diagnostic errors.
- Before any authorized repair, preserve backups and relevant audit records.
- A completed diagnostic means the probe ran; it does not certify compliance, ownership, or health.

Official starting points: https://support.apple.com/mac and https://support.apple.com/guide/deployment/welcome/web .
